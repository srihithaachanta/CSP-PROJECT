Community Health Guidelines
  • Boil Drinking Water: Always boil municipal tap water for at least 3 minutes before consumption during monsoon rains.
  • Clear Stagnant Basins: Empty discarded tires, coconut shells, and open drums to prevent mosquito breeding grounds.
  • Unclog Household Drains: Keep household outflow drains free from solid garbage to avoid back-up during rains.
  • Report Leaks Immediately: Use JalaMitra photo upload to report leaking pipes before contaminants seep in.
